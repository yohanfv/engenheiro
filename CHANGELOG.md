# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- Race start: the engineer stays quiet while you wait on the grid, while the lights come on and for 20 seconds
  after lights out (only "lights out" and urgent calls get through). What he had to say comes after that, updated
  ("Start done, P3...").
- With the formation lap on, he does the briefing and the radio check during the formation lap and goes quiet when
  you line up on the grid. Without the formation lap there is no radio check on the grid.
- New option in Settings: "Quiet at the start" (on by default).
- Demo race: a short formation phase, the five start lights and lights out.

## 1.0.4

- Fixed: after a Safety Car, the engineer could say that several drivers "lost 20 seconds" and "may have a problem"
  (the slow lap was the Safety Car lap). Laps run partly under the Safety Car or VSC are now ignored.
- Demo race: the cars wait on the grid while the engineer does the opening radio, so the calls match what you see.
- Clearer answer when you ask about tyres right after a stop.

## 1.0.3

- A "☕ Support" button at the top of the dashboard and a short note on the waiting screen, linking to Ko-fi.
  Nothing changes while you drive: no pop-ups, no voice messages, nothing locked.

## 1.0.2

- Support link: the About screen and the README now have a link to leave an optional tip on Ko-fi.
  The app stays free; nothing is locked.

## 1.0.1

- Fixed: in the downloaded version, the demo drew the cars off the track (it could not find the track maps).
- Fixed: the demo no longer saves corner and sector marks, so it can't mix up the map of a real session.
  Wrong marks saved by 1.0.0 are detected and thrown away automatically.
- Fixed: the first time the app opened, the window could show "localhost refused to connect".

## 1.0.0 - first public release

- Voice race engineer for F1 25 (PS5, Xbox, PC) in English and Brazilian Portuguese.
- Race radio, gaps like official timing, overtakes and position changes, Safety Car and weather calls.
- Strategy from real tyre wear (no-stop, one-stop, two-stop), pit calls and Safety Car re-planning.
- Track watch: retirements with location and reason, slow/stopped cars, contact, rival wing damage, drivers
  losing time, rival pit predictions, undercut and overcut calls.
- Free questions by microphone (wake word "Engineer" + confirmation beep), controller buttons or text.
- Live dashboard (also on phone), practice analysis, corner-by-corner report, session history.
- Demo mode, welcome screen, About screen, option to keep the dashboard on this PC only.
- Tells you when a new version is available (can be turned off in Settings).
